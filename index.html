02My Story

I didn't change who I was. I changed what I did every day.

For twenty years I built a career in the events industry: long nights, constant travel, deadlines that never stopped. I was good at my job and proud of it. I also weighed 125 kg. Like most men in that position, I kept telling myself I'd start once work calmed down. It never calmed down.

The change didn't come from motivation. It came from a decision that some things were no longer up for negotiation: training, eating clean, 8–10K steps a day, sleep and recovery. I didn't need to be perfect. I needed to keep showing up, early in the morning, before the day took over, with my wife beside me and my three daughters watching.

Today I'm 44, athletic, a SQUATWOLF Athlete Ambassador, and still running a full career. I never found extra time. I organised the time I already had. Now I help men over 40 do the same, because you can buy almost anything in life, but you can't buy your health back.

06Questions

Message on Instagram
I'm over 40. Isn't it too late for me?

No. I started my own transformation in my late 30s at 125 kg. Your body responds to training and good nutrition at any age. It just needs the right approach for where you are now.

I barely have time. Will this work with my schedule?

The protocol is built for busy men. We plan around your work, travel and family. Time is rarely the problem; organisation is.

Do I need to follow a strict diet?

No crash diets and no tasteless meals. You'll learn the basics of protein, carbs and fats, and build balanced meals you actually enjoy.

Do I need a gym membership?

A gym helps, but it isn't required to start. Your plan is built around the equipment and environment you have access to.

Is coaching online or in person?

Coaching is delivered online so it works wherever you are. We'll cover the details on your discovery call.

What happens on the discovery call?

We talk about your goals, your current lifestyle and what's held you back. If it's a fit, I'll explain how we'd work together. No pressure, no hard sell.
