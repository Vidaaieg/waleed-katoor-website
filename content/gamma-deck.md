# The plan on one page

Waleed went from roughly 125 kg to athletic at 44 while running a director-level job and raising three daughters. He isn't selling fitness. He is **proof that a busy man can recover his health**.

Today that story doesn't turn into business: 1,209 posts, 1,232 followers, no website, no booking path, no client system. But the account is moving: the last 30 days ran at 1,023 median views against 827 before. **This is a build, not a rescue.**

- **Social media** creates demand: 4 Reels a week + a Saturday carousel, Arabic-first, him on camera
- **Website** converts: one page, eight sections, a calendar wired into automation
- **CRM** runs the coaching: dashboard, client profiles, progress, payments, voice notes
- **AI automation** removes the admin: nine client automations + a leads engine

---

# Five decisions that shape everything

1. **Proof, not coach.** He shares what worked on him and sends people to specialists for the details.
2. **He speaks.** Reels where he talks earn nearly twice the views (1,622 vs 822) and 3× the likes.
3. **Arabic first.** His growth audience thinks in Arabic. Captions burned into every video.
4. **The client enters the data, not Waleed.** Forms and WhatsApp do the typing.
5. **Channels before automation.** WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook are set up properly first.

**30-day targets:** 1,500 median views (from 859) · 65% talking-head Reels (from 13%) · under 10% zero-comment Reels (from 34%) · following:follower ratio 1.0 (from 1.71)

# CRM: coaching from a phone, without typing

**Design principles:** the client enters the data · one screen a day · voice over typing.

**Dashboard · الرئيسية:** subscriptions (active, ending within 14 days, expired, upgrade candidates) · follow-up (needs follow-up today, no check-in this week, new InBody to review, AI drafts waiting) · clients (search, new leads, booked calls) · headline numbers: active clients, renewals, revenue, new leads.

**Customer profile · ملف العميل:** today's weight against starting weight at the top · photo, plan, status · WhatsApp, email, Instagram · start and renewal dates · weight chart, height, InBody history · weekly photo timeline · meal PDF with version history · check-in log · transcribed voice notes · payments.

The photo timeline is the product: it shows progress, makes the case for renewal and, with permission, becomes content.

---

# CRM: forms, payments, notes and data

**Registration form · استمارة التسجيل:** about you, body baseline, health (allergies, diseases, medications, injuries), history, and separate consents. **Medical flag:** any declared condition or medication blocks AI meal plans and triggers a doctor-clearance request.

**Weekly check-in:** weight · photo · training days · food adherence 1–10 · sleep · note. A missed check-in puts him on the dashboard list.

**Payments and renewals · الدفعات والتجديد:** paid / due / overdue, full history, a renewal pipeline starting 14 days out.

**Notes register and Telegram:** Waleed sends a voice note, it's transcribed, matched to the client and saved. A private Telegram bot answers "who hasn't checked in?" and sends a morning digest.

**Leads pipeline:** New lead → Engaged → Call booked → Client → Not now.

**Data protection:** health data handled under Saudi Arabia's PDPL: explicit consent, encrypted, access limited, exportable and deletable.

# The leads engine

1. **Post + CTA:** e.g. اكتب «بروتوكول» بالكومنت وببعتلك القاعدة كاملة
2. **Auto DM:** the promised resource + a website link unique to that person
3. **Click = lead:** the CRM registers his handle, the source post and the date
4. **Assistant:** answers repeat questions, qualifies, offers the calendar
5. **Booked or follow-up:** call booked, or queued for a human

Because every link is unique, we know **which post produced which client**, not only views.

**Follow-up within the rules:** Meta limits business messages to a window after the person's last message, and WhatsApp needs opt-in. So follow-up moves to WhatsApp with consent, happens personally from the "Not now" list, or comes back through new content. No mass cold DMs.

**Stack:** n8n (self-hosted) · WhatsApp Cloud API · Meta Graph API · an Arabic-strong LLM · vision for InBody · Whisper-class transcription · Cal.com · Telegram Bot API · branded PDFs.

**Rollout:** Month 1 catch every lead · Month 2 run the clients · Month 3 the intelligence layer.

# Measurement and guardrails

**Funnel we report monthly:** DMs → leads (tracked clicks) → qualified → calls booked (and show rate) → clients closed → retention and renewals. Monthly report + 30-minute review: keep, change, stop.

**Guardrails**
- **The medical line:** no medical claims, no supplement protocols, no calorie prescriptions. The back story is told as story only.
- **His dialect, not ours:** Waleed reads every Arabic line aloud before it goes public.
- **Consent:** client photos published only with separate written permission, withdrawable.
- **Client data:** Saudi PDPL, encrypted, access-limited.
- **Platform rules:** Meta-approved templates, opt-in, no mass unsolicited DMs.
- **Honest numbers:** every public claim is true and checkable.
